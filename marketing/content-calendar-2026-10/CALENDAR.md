## Four priorities

### Direct sales
Two products, one price each, no discounts. So sales come from removing friction and repeating the path to order: a How to order post in launch week and again at payday, a benefits post in allowed words, the mirror revealed on its own in launch week and detailed mid-month, a gifting post as Q4 opens, and a link sticker in stories every day from 3 October. Sales posts sit on the two payday windows (1–4 and 27–31 October).
Posts: P07, P08, P09, P10, P11, P12, P15, P18, P21, P22, P24, P25, P27, P29, P32, P33, P35

### Brand awareness
Reels carry the ambience. The launch film, the eight-piece sequence, the sound of the ritual and the one-take evening ritual are built to be watched by people who have never heard the name. Covers alternate cream, forest and beige so the grid reads as one house.
Posts: P01, P02, P03, P04, P05, P06, P07, P10, P20, P21, P23, P26, P28, P34, P36

### Audience growth
Carousels earn saves and repeat delivery, so the method series (the order of a ritual, roller or gua sha, what the ritual does, care) is where new followers come from. Every reel opens with a specific first line, and captions carry search terms: skincare ritual, gua sha, jade roller, cleansing brush, vanity mirror, Dubai, UAE.
Posts: P13, P14, P15, P16, P17, P19, P20, P23, P28, P30, P31, P33

### Creator collaborations
Two gifting waves, each landing two Collab posts on Noyau's grid. Collab posts reach both audiences and cost Noyau only an approval. Wave one is dispatched with the launch, wave two mid-month. One or two paid creators in wave two, with usage rights.
Posts: P14, P19, P26, P31, P32

## Voice and claims
Rules:
- British spelling. No exclamation marks. No emoji.
- The brand speaks as a house. No founder, no I, no behind-the-scenes face.
- Short declarative lines, often with a full stop. Concrete nouns over adjectives: Saffiano leather, natural jade, food-grade silicone.
- One price, everywhere. Never a discount, a sale, a code or a countdown to a price.
- Sign off every caption and DM: With care, Noyau
- Logo without the ® mark until the filing is confirmed.

Allowed: depuffing · lifting · plumping · boosts collagen · boosts circulation · boosts absorption · helps with acne treatment (never treats acne) · hypoallergenic (occasionally) · dermatologist-approved (occasionally)

Avoided: anti-ageing · firming · tightening · draining · detox · clinically tested · antibacterial · glow · miracle · transform · must-have · holy grail · sonic · clean · eco-friendly · sustainable · discount, sale, offer, code

Claims to hold back:
- Dermatologist-approved and hypoallergenic are cleared for occasional use. In October each appears once: hypoallergenic in Asked this week on 16 October, dermatologist-approved in What the ritual does on 28 October.
- Collagen and acne wording reads as a health claim under UAE advertising rules. Keep both out of paid posts until an adviser confirms the wording, and out of organic posts in October.
